# Phase 34R — Contact Product Depth, Sharing Structure & Engagement

> Adds the next layer of tangible user value to the contact product: better
> contact health, richer public card analytics, and more structured books and
> permissions.

## Phase status
Pre-plan

## Portfolio priority
5 of 5 (`34O-34S`)

## Phase objective
Deepen the day-to-day usefulness of Kontax beyond storage and sync by improving
contact quality workflows, book organization, and share visibility.

1. P34R-01 — surface contact quality gaps
2. P34R-02 — make public cards measurable
3. P34R-03 — expose richer book structure
4. P34R-04 — add book-level permission controls

## Why this phase sits here

Phase 34R is intentionally later because it is mostly expansionary product
depth rather than core trust/hardening work. It becomes more valuable once:

- sync behavior is legible
- support/admin operations are steadier
- the platform hardening work in 34P is in place
