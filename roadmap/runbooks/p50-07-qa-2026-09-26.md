## Accessibility — axe-core, WCAG 2.0/2.1 A+AA and 2.2 AA tags, at 1440 and 375

All 14 pages above: **0 violations**, after one fix (below).

- Fixed: `/developers` at 375 — code blocks and API tables scroll sideways but weren't
  keyboard-focusable (`scrollable-region-focusable`, 24 nodes). They now take `tabIndex=0`
  (tables also get `role="region"` + a label).
- Contrast: every text token pair is ≥ 4.5:1 (values recorded in the header of
  `src/app/(marketing)/_components/marketing.css`); `--mkt-g400` is decorative only.
- Signature diagram: one `role="img"` with a text alternative; drawing is `aria-hidden`.
- Reduced motion: under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (and with Kontax motion "off", or
  JS disabled) the signature renders its final frame with no animation — verified by
  checking the merged card's computed opacity is 1 at load in each case.
- Keyboard: mobile menu opens with Enter (`aria-expanded="true"`), first Tab lands on
  "Features", Escape closes it and returns focus to the menu button. Pricing toggle switches
  Monthly → Annual by click and keyboard.
- Horizontal scroll: none on any page at 375 or 1440 (`scrollWidth === innerWidth`).

  pricing matrix + plan card, security, privacy sub-processors).

  are help-centre how-tos (DAVx5, PWA install) — instructions, not a sync claim.

  while /security claims only secrets are sealed at rest. Both are pre-existing copy.
